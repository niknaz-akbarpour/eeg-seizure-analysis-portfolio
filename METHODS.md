# Methods

## Design

Retrospective exploratory analysis of a deliberately small subset of a public, de-identified EEG dataset.

## Record selection

Two seizure-containing EDF recordings were selected from each of three participants:

- `chb01_03.edf`, `chb01_04.edf`
- `chb02_16.edf`, `chb02_16+.edf`
- `chb03_01.edf`, `chb03_02.edf`

Selection was based on the presence of an annotated seizure and was performed for a manageable portfolio-scale analysis. It was not random and does not represent the complete database.

## Window construction

Annotated seizure intervals were divided into non-overlapping 5-second windows. For each recording, three times as many non-seizure windows were sampled without replacement. Candidate non-seizure windows were required to remain at least 60 seconds away from every annotated seizure interval in the recording.

## Signal preparation

Each window was mean-centred channel by channel and filtered from 0.5 to 40 Hz with a fourth-order Butterworth band-pass filter applied forward and backward. Features were aggregated across available bipolar scalp-EEG channels so that the pilot models did not depend on one channel name.

## Feature extraction

Time-domain features included RMS amplitude, variance, line length, zero-crossing rate, and kurtosis. Frequency-domain features were calculated from Welch power spectral density estimates and included total power, spectral entropy, relative band power, and selected band-power ratios.

## Models

- Logistic regression with standardized features and balanced class weights
- Random forest with 400 trees, a minimum leaf size of two, and balanced-subsample class weights

Hyperparameters were fixed before evaluation. No separate hyperparameter search was performed.

## Validation

Leave-one-participant-out cross-validation created three folds. Each participant served once as the complete test set while both other participants formed the training set. The classification threshold was fixed at 0.5.

## Outcomes

Reported metrics were balanced accuracy, sensitivity/recall, specificity, precision, F1, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and confusion-matrix counts. Both fold-specific and pooled held-out predictions are retained in the `results` folder.

## Reproducibility

The random seed was fixed at `20261014`. All feature rows, fold predictions, metrics, and figures are generated by `run_analysis.py`.
